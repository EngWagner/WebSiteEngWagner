01 · About Public

Engineering, security and a foot in entrepreneurship

I started in IT in mid-2014 as a computer technician and later moved to technical support. Since then I have worked on the service desk of a multinational, earned my degree in Computer Engineering, moved into software development for good and completed two postgraduate programs.

Today I build with .NET, Next.js and PostgreSQL for a laboratory with branches in Brazil and Paraguay — from web and mobile to infrastructure — with AI agents in my daily work. After hours, I run my own ventures — the Uaus store, iMenu and Alphamira — and study the financial markets.

This site follows what I stand for at work: the professional side is public; the personal side lives in the secure zone, with verified and logged access.
